PREVALENCE OF AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDER

About 1 in 31 (3.2%) children aged 8 years has been identified with autism
spectrum disorder according to estimates from CDC's Autism and Developmental
Disabilities Monitoring (ADDM) Network. This is the current figure.

ASD is reported to occur in all racial, ethnic and socioeconomic groups.

ASD is over 3 times more common among boys than among girls.

About 1 in 6 (17%) children aged 3 to 17 years were diagnosed with a
developmental disability, as reported by parents, during 2009 to 2017. These
included ASD, attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder, blindness and cerebral
palsy, among others.

Prevalence is the number of people in a population that have a condition,
relative to all the people in that population. It is usually shown as a
percentage or a proportion.

IDENTIFIED PREVALENCE OVER TIME, ADDM NETWORK

Surveillance year 2022, birth year 2014, 16 sites: 32.2 per 1,000 children,
which is about 1 in 31 children. This is the current figure.
Surveillance year 2020, birth year 2012: 27.6 per 1,000, about 1 in 36.
Surveillance year 2018, birth year 2010: 23.0 per 1,000, about 1 in 44.
Surveillance year 2016, birth year 2008: 18.5 per 1,000, about 1 in 54.
Surveillance year 2014, birth year 2006: 16.8 per 1,000, about 1 in 59.
Surveillance year 2012, birth year 2004: 14.5 per 1,000, about 1 in 69.
Surveillance year 2010, birth year 2002: 14.7 per 1,000, about 1 in 68.
Surveillance year 2008, birth year 2000: 11.3 per 1,000, about 1 in 88.
Surveillance year 2006, birth year 1998: 9.0 per 1,000, about 1 in 110.
Surveillance year 2004, birth year 1996: 8.0 per 1,000, about 1 in 125.
Surveillance year 2002, birth year 1994: 6.6 per 1,000, about 1 in 150.
Surveillance year 2000, birth year 1992: 6.7 per 1,000, about 1 in 150.

NOTE ON OUT OF DATE FIGURES

The figure 1 in 44 corresponds to the 2018 surveillance year and is out of date.
The figure 1 in 36 corresponds to 2020 and is also superseded. When asked how
common autism is, the correct current answer is about 1 in 31 children aged 8
years, based on the 2022 surveillance year.
